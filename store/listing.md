## Full description

**Budgeting for people who don't get the same paycheque every month.**

Most money apps assume you're on a salary. Pence is built for hourly and shift workers — nurses, carers, warehouse, retail, hospitality, agency staff — whose pay changes every single week.

**Log your shifts, see your real pay**
Tap in the hours you actually worked, including overtime and shift premiums. Pence works out this week's take-home after Income Tax and National Insurance — before your payslip even arrives.

**Did you get paid right?**
Underpayment is common in hourly work, and most people never notice. Pence tells you exactly what your payslip *should* say — gross, tax, NI, pension, student loan, take-home — so you can compare it to the real one in seconds.

**A savings goal with a finish date**
Not just a number: "You'll hit £2,000 by March." Pick a weekly or monthly amount, tick it off each week, and watch the date come closer. Pence even shows how much sooner you'd get there by picking up one extra shift a month.

**Plan ahead**
Set your expenses, add extra income like child benefit or a side hustle, and see month-by-month exactly where your money goes and how your savings grow — with interest.

**Private by design**
No account. No bank connection. No adverts. No tracking. Everything stays on your phone, and works offline.

Uses 2026/27 UK tax rates (England, Wales & Northern Ireland).
