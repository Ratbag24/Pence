Privacy Policy

Pence · Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version: Pence doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Pence stores

Pence saves the information you type in — your hourly rate, hours, shifts, expenses, savings amounts and payslip checks — so it's there next time you open the app. This is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Pence has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We have no servers that receive your data, so there is nothing for us to see, sell or lose.

Internet access

The app works fully offline. The web version at getpence.app loads its own files from the site you visit and nothing else.

Backups

If you use Export backup, Pence creates a file on your device containing your data. Where that file goes (a note, an email, a cloud drive) is entirely up to you and is handled by your device's share sheet, not by Pence.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset everything, or simply uninstall the app. Because nothing leaves your device, that removes everything.

Children

Pence doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the Pence GitHub repository.

Pence is a planning tool, not financial advice. Tax figures are estimates based on standard UK rates.
